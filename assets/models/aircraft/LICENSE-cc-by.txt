This is discharged in game by the **credits screen**: `C` on the character-select
screen (`src/game/credits.rs`). The aircraft block is drawn first and brightest
because it is the part that is legally required; everything else on that screen
is courtesy.

The screen uses `assets/fonts/DejaVuSansMono.ttf` rather than Bevy's built-in
font, which has no glyphs for "ě" or "á" and drew them as empty boxes.
